About Bajpayee Kachodi Bhandar

Located on Naval Kishore Road, near Cathedral School and opposite Bank of India in Hazratganj, Bajpayee Kachodi Bhandar is one of Lucknow's most loved breakfast destinations. Students, office-goers, shoppers and visitors to the city all start their day here.

The recipe has stayed simple and unchanged for decades: freshly fried kachodi and khasta, served with a bold, spicy aloo-chole sabzi. Affordable, filling and full of flavour — that is why the morning queue never gets shorter.
